## Goal
The original "sharp" idea — an on-chain mint where the chain's `totalSupply` becomes
the global ledger.

## Research
Lower priority: the off-chain `personal_sign` certificate already carries the point
(pay/commit to "own" something never original) for free, no gas. An on-chain mint adds
cost and friction for a mostly-symbolic gain. Keep as an optional collector tier if
ever wanted.
