## Goal
Let people inspect a work enlarged and deliberately certify it, then keep their
certified works as images.

## Delivered
Full-screen inspect modal (serial, coordinate, fingerprint, live claim status).
Certify from the card; your certificates live in the "Certified · you" drawer, each
exportable as a high-resolution PNG (the work + serial + coordinate + fingerprint,
wallet address when present). Certificates persist in localStorage.
