## Goal
Decide what happens when a browser-id owner clears their cache: their certificate is
gone, but the work stays locked forever by a "ghost."

## Research
A cost of exclusivity backed by a weak (losable) identity. It's slow, and arguably
thematic ("you lost the proof — it's gone, like the works you didn't save"). If it
ever sterilises the space, the fix is wallet-only exclusivity (a portable identity),
with browser-id demoted to a non-exclusive keepsake.

## Open questions
- Leave it (thematic), or move exclusivity to wallet-only?
