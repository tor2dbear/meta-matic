## Goal
Visualise the latent field as one shared object: the machine's single path, where
everyone has planted a flag, and your own marks — "one path, everyone walks it."

## Delivered
Full-bleed band that flex-grows to fill the viewport (footer pinned), background
matched to the section above. Renders the shared walk, the machine's now, the recent
trail, your own certificates (blue rings), and — since exclusive certificates
shipped — **everyone's certified points** as a faint constellation (`GET /claimed`).
Isotropic square projection with a dot lattice that dissolves at the edges (the field
continues past the window).
